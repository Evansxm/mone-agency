Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a model application to Mone Agency, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name, age, email address, and Instagram handle
  • Physical measurements: height, bust/chest, waist, hips, shoe size
  • Hair and eye colour
  • Preferred scouting location (Sandton, Cape Town, or Pietermaritzburg)
  • Digital photograph uploads (polaroids/digitals)

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the purpose of evaluating your modeling application and potential representation by Mone Agency. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

  • To review and assess your application
  • To communicate with you regarding your application status
  • To connect you with casting and booking opportunities

Data Storage and Security

Application form submissions are emailed directly to mone.agency@mail.com via automated processing. Mone Agency takes reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Photographs submitted are reviewed internally and are not publicly displayed without your explicit consent.

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies for site functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at mone.agency@mail.com or via WhatsApp at +27 83 382 6027.

Third-Party Services

Form submissions are processed via Google Apps Script and sent directly to the agency email. Our website hosts images and assets via GitHub Pages.
